
Data Acquisition

Data sampling requires specific adaptations of the acquisition program
to the A/D board in use. Any technical changes of A/D boards would
require new adaptations of the software. Therefore, NEUROLAB does not
support data acquisition but processes any data sampled by the
commercially available software package TurboLab. (TurboLab is
distributed by Bressner Technology, Grobenzell FRG; Scentech Inc,
Woburn MA, USA and as Globalab by Data Translation, Marlboro MA,
USA). Turbolab conveniently supports data sampling with a variety of
A/D boards from Analogic, Analog Devices, Data Translation, IO-Tech
and Stemmer, with different numbers of channels, sampling rates and
amplitude resolution.

The program is adapted to binary data files in the TurboLab 3.0 format
as well as the new TurboLab 4.2 format. It processes data sampled for
a maximum number of 16 channels and with a maximum of 16 bit amplitude
resolution. In Turbolab 3.0 each file consists of a 512 byte header
with the main information and comments on the sample run. Thereafter,
the data of all channels follow sequentially in two-byte integer
format. Thus, even if data has been sampled by another software, it
should be easy to transform the data file in the Turbolab 3.0 format.

The organization of the Turbolab 4.2 header is more complicated. It is
designed to cope with variable data structures and future developments
of A/D boards and sampling processes.


Turbolab 3.0 Header

(TYPE-Definition for strg10=string[10];)
				{Length of the string10 is 11 byte}

Header = Record
ID:string[8];				{identification="Turbolab"}
NChan:byte;				      {number of channels}
Gain:array[0..15] of byte;		{gain of channels}
Names:array[0..15] of strg10;		{name of channels}
FrqKts:strg10;				{sampl. freq. per channel, 11 bytes}
FrqSts:Strg10;				{total sampling freq. 11 bytes}
Bufferlen:strg[9];			{total no. of data, 10 bytes}
Year:integer;
Month:byte;
Day:byte;
Hour:byte;
Minute:byte;
Label:String[64];			{data protocol, 65 bytes}
Board:byte;				{board identification no. 1:DT-2821}
					 {2:DT-2821F-16-SE, 3:DT-2821F-8DI}
					 {4:DT2827, 5:DT-2828}
Base:integer;				{base-address}
DMAKan1,DMAKan2:byte;			{1. and 2. DMA channel}
IntLev:byte;				{interrupt level}
InpRange:byte;				{input-range}
Coding:byte;				{coding: 1=straight binary}
					 {2=offset binary 3=two's complement}
InpMode:byte;				{1=single ended, 2=differential}
FFU:array[315..512] of byte;		{for future improvements}
END;
